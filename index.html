Saints, Fathers & Heresies

From the Crucifixion to the present — the saints who shaped the Church, the heresies that provoked much of its doctrine, and the five ages that organize the story.

Dates are traditional and, for the earliest centuries, often approximate or disputed. Sainthood in the Patristic Age was mostly informal, popular recognition rather than formal canonization; later periods reflect the formal process as it developed.
